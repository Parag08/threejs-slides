How it works

Architecture

Three layers, one world

stage

HTML on top

A fixed 1920×1080 stage that scales to any screen without reflowing.

scene

3D behind

Each slide owns a scene, coloured from the theme's CSS variables.

journey

Camera between

Changing slides flies the camera along a path through space.

Journeys

Every slide is a place.

Pick how the camera travels: drift through space, track sideways, dive down, spiral, or stay still and cross-fade.

Five presses step through the five journey types on the ring.
Data

Charts that grow into place

The bars scene reads its numbers from a JSON attribute on the slide.
